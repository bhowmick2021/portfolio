For consulting clients

Three ways to start.

Selective fixed-scope and part-time engagements; hours and overlap are set per engagement. No fixed percentage is promised, because benefit depends on your workload.

Selected work delivered at Radix Web Services. The services below are scoped with you for each engagement.

01

BigQuery Cost & Performance Review

Problem. Spend is climbing, ownership is unclear, and it is not obvious which changes matter most.

Deliverables. Prioritized cost drivers; proposed query, table and schedule changes; expected benefit with stated assumptions, estimated from your workload; implementation scope. Optional follow-on: an agreed optimization sprint.

02

Data Workflow Automation Sprint

Problem. A repeated reporting, reconciliation or data-preparation task consumes recurring hours.

Deliverables. One agreed workflow (Python, n8n, APIs, AI assistance where suitable) with exception handling, operating instructions and handover. The outcome measure (hours, turnaround or errors) is set at scoping.

03

Analytics Platform Health Check

Problem. A lean data team has fragile BigQuery/Tableau reporting, duplicated logic or unclear dependencies.

Deliverables. A focused assessment, the priority reliability and dependency issues, and a practical remediation plan. Optional follow-on: bounded stabilization or part-time governance support.
