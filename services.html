Quick Audit

$50 – $150

A 48-hour review of your cloud spend, data platform, or database setup. You get a written findings report: what's costing money, what's fragile, and what to fix first. No commitment beyond the audit itself.

⏱ 48 hours Best for: a first, low-risk way to work together

Optimization Sprint

$150 – $2,000

The actual doing work: database builds, migrations, automation scripts, small custom tools, or a fix identified in a Quick Audit. Priced by scope once we know what's involved.

⏱ 2–7 days, depending on scope Best for: a defined piece of work with a clear deliverable

Platform Advisory

Scoped after a conversation

For larger initiatives that need discovery before a fixed scope makes sense: platform modernization, data governance and metadata frameworks, or ongoing FinOps programs. Priced as a project fee or a light-touch retainer.
