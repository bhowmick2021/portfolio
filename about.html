About

Abhijeet Bhowmick

Enterprise data platform and cloud analytics consultant, 19 years in, now opening an independent practice alongside a full-time role.

My Story

I've spent 19 years in data, starting in reporting and business intelligence and moving into full platform ownership: migrations, modernization, automation, and governance. By day, I run enterprise data platforms on Google Cloud and BigQuery for a company with users across India, APAC and Europe.

I'm now opening that experience up as an independent consulting practice alongside my full-time role. It's early. This is where the practice starts, not where it ends. I'm selective about what I take on because I'm doing this around a full-time job, but the depth behind every engagement is real, built over two decades of actually running the kind of platforms I now help other teams fix.

How I Work

🧩 Small, clearly scoped work with a defined deliverable, so you know what you're getting before we start.
💬 Upfront pricing: no surprise invoices.
🧾 Clean handover: documented, reusable, no "figure it out yourself" files.
🔍 I optimize for clarity and maintainability, not cleverness, and I'll tell you honestly if a task needs more than a quick fix.

Principles

  • I only claim what I can back up. If a result came from my day job rather than a client engagement, I say so.
  • I'll tell you if something isn't a fit, even if it costs me the work.
  • No scope creep disguised as "while we're at it."
  • Fixed prices are fixed. Advisory work gets scoped honestly before we start, not padded after.
