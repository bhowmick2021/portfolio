AI-assisted data platform and governance delivery

One connected 2026 delivery: Query Doctor, Atlas, Governance Hub and a shared backend.

Period2026
RolePlatform owner: problem, architecture, validation, rollout
StackFastAPI, Docker/Compose, Python, BigQuery, Google sign-in, GPT, Claude
AttributionDelivered at Radix Web Services

Problem

Cost, ownership, dependencies and platform health were not visible, and the supporting tools were scattered.

My contribution

  • Problem definition, architecture, stakeholder alignment, validation and rollout are mine.
  • GPT and Claude assisted coding, debugging, tests, documentation and iteration, and I review every change.

Decisions

  • Consolidate separate governance apps onto one shared FastAPI backend: simpler hosting and deployment, plus a data-freshness API.
  • An attention-first console for Governance Hub.
  • Atlas pivoted from a catalog to a lineage and ownership platform, released first as a gated production pilot.
  • Stage-then-production validation for Query Doctor VNext.

Delivered change

  • Governance Hub: daily governance scans with findings in an authenticated internal platform.
  • Query Doctor VNext: SQL analysis, formatting and highlighting, and cost estimation before a query runs.
  • Atlas: lineage, ownership and health visibility, in production pilot behind Google sign-in.
  • Shared governance backend: one FastAPI service in Docker behind the tools.

Validation

Backend verified in staging and production; Query Doctor VNext validated on stage before production; releases carry documented validation and rollback checkpoints.

Approved outcome

One place to see cost, ownership and platform health, with operational support consolidated. No productivity or "AI saved X" claims are made.

Not claimed

Atlas is a pilot, not general availability, and its scheduled refresh is paused. No speed-up percentages from AI.
