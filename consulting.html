Bounded review

BigQuery Cost & Performance Review

Problem. Spend is climbing, ownership is unclear and it is not obvious which changes matter most.

Deliverables. Prioritized cost drivers; proposed query, table and schedule changes; expected benefit with stated assumptions (estimated from your workload); implementation scope.

Access needed. Read access to BigQuery job metadata and billing export for the agreed projects, plus a sample of the heaviest queries.

Exclusions. No guaranteed saving percentage; no production changes without a written agreement.

Small-to-medium implementation

Data Workflow Automation Sprint

Problem. A repeated reporting, reconciliation or data-preparation task consumes recurring hours.

Deliverables. One agreed workflow (Python, n8n, APIs, AI assistance where suitable) with exception handling, operating instructions and handover; outcome measure (hours, turnaround or errors) set at scoping.

Access needed. The current manual process, sample data, and credentials for the agreed systems issued by you with least privilege.

Exclusions. No 24x7 support and no ongoing ownership unless separately agreed.

Bounded review

Analytics Platform Health Check

Problem. A lean data team has fragile BigQuery/Tableau reporting, duplicated logic or unclear dependencies.

Deliverables. Focused assessment, priority reliability and dependency issues, practical remediation plan. Optional follow-on: a bounded stabilization piece.

Access needed. Read access to the agreed reporting layer, dataset and extract lists, and an hour with the people who maintain them.

My Journey

Business problems, interventions and outcomes

Examples from my employer role at Radix Web Services. They show how I work, not an independent client history or a firm.

  1. Internal Radix employer work

    BigQuery cost governance

    Problem. Spend was rising and drivers were unclear.
    Intervention. Tuned queries and workloads, partitioning and clustering, lifecycle fixes, an itemized audit and leadership cost visibility.
    Outcome. 15–30% normalized BigQuery cost reduction (Aug 2025–Jan 2026), BigQuery only. Case →

  2. Internal Radix employer work

    Slow Tableau extracts

    Problem. Very large extracts took hours on BigQuery.
    Intervention. A secondary reporting database with a monitored daily sync.
    Outcome. Approximately 95% reduction in runtime for one Tableau extract view in a benchmark. Case →

  3. Internal Radix employer work

    Invisible cost, ownership and health

    Problem. Teams could not see who owns data, what depends on what, or where cost goes.
    Intervention. Query Doctor, Atlas and a daily governance scan on one backend, built with GPT and Claude under my review.
    Outcome. One view of cost, ownership and platform health; Atlas is a production pilot. Case →
