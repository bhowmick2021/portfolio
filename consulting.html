Services and deliverables

Bounded reviews and small-to-medium implementation

Scope, duration and price are agreed after confirming your environment and access. No fixed percentage is promised; benefit depends on your workload.

Bounded review

BigQuery Cost & Performance Review

Problem
Spend is climbing, ownership is unclear and it is not obvious which changes matter most.
Deliverables
Prioritized cost drivers; proposed query, table and schedule changes; expected benefit with stated assumptions (estimated from your workload); implementation scope.
Access needed
Read access to BigQuery job metadata and billing export for the agreed projects, plus a sample of the heaviest queries.
Exclusions
No guaranteed saving percentage; no production changes without a written agreement.

Small-to-medium implementation

Data Workflow Automation Sprint

Problem
A repeated reporting, reconciliation or data-preparation task consumes recurring hours.
Deliverables
One agreed workflow (Python, n8n, APIs, AI assistance where suitable) with exception handling, operating instructions and handover; outcome measure (hours, turnaround or errors) set at scoping.
Access needed
The current manual process, sample data, and credentials for the agreed systems issued by you with least privilege.
Exclusions
No 24x7 support and no ongoing ownership unless separately agreed.

Bounded review

Analytics Platform Health Check

Problem
A lean data team has fragile BigQuery/Tableau reporting, duplicated logic or unclear dependencies.
Deliverables
Focused assessment, priority reliability and dependency issues, practical remediation plan. Optional follow-on: a bounded stabilization piece.
Access needed
Read access to the agreed reporting layer, dataset and extract lists, and an hour with the people who maintain them.
Exclusions
Not a security or compliance audit; no promise to rebuild the platform.
