BigQuery to TimescaleDB reporting platform

Hours-long Tableau extracts served from a secondary database.

PeriodBenchmark Jun 2026 · rebuild Aug–Sep 2026
RoleDesigned, built and rebuilt; owned cutover validation
StackBigQuery, TimescaleDB/PostgreSQL, Airflow, Python, Tableau, Slack
AttributionDelivered at Radix Web Services

Problem

Very large Tableau extracts took hours on BigQuery, and the daily sync that fed reporting ran for hours in its legacy form.

My contribution

  • Designed and built a secondary TimescaleDB/PostgreSQL database fed by a daily Airflow sync.
  • Rebuilt the sync in 2026 for reliability and owned cutover validation.

Decisions

  • Serve extracts from a secondary database instead of repeated heavy BigQuery reads.
  • Atomic-transaction safety on the sync and Slack alerting on failure.
  • Rebuild with a larger test suite and validate at cutover.

Delivered change

  • Daily Airflow sync, in production since Jul 2026, with near-zero marginal query cost.
  • A rebuilt sync released Aug–Sep 2026.
  • Two production data-integrity bugs found and fixed on the first live run: a string-escaping bug that silently disabled incremental decompression, and a null-value validation bug that starved the job queue.

Validation

Benchmark of one large extract view (Jun 2026). For the rebuild: expanded tests, then cutover validation, which surfaced the two bugs above.

Approved outcome

Benchmark: approximately 95% reduction in runtime for one Tableau extract view in a benchmark (BigQuery vs TimescaleDB, Jun 2026), with near-zero marginal query cost.

Two records

Platform and benchmark

Jun 2026 benchmark, in production since Jul 2026. One Tableau extract view: approximately 95% reduction in runtime (BigQuery vs TimescaleDB). Near-zero marginal query cost.

2026 rebuild

27 Aug – 7 Sep 2026. Atomic-transaction safety, larger test suite, cutover validation, two bugs fixed.

Not claimed

The benchmark is one view, not an average, and does not apply to the rebuild. Not a general latency claim.
